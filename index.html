About Rob

I grew up in Duluth, Minnesota, learning music in a public school program built on drilling the fundamentals until they were second nature. I’ve lived and taught in Melbourne for nearly 40 years.

Musician friends call me Bob — hence Bobby Blue, the host of the Book of Riffs channel. The videos share the simple rules I use to get horn sections riffing together, and the scale outlines I use to turn jazz standards into solos.

How I teach improvisation

Three things, in this order, so students can play something real from the first session.

one

The blues

The form everything grows from. Guide tones, call and response, and hearing where you are in the twelve bars.

two

Riffing

Count Basie–style jump-blues riffs: simple rules for 3rd, 5th and 7th voices so a whole section can make up backing figures on the spot.

three

Barry Harris harmony

Scale outlines and 6th-diminished sounds, adapted for horn players, that turn the chords of a standard into lines you can actually play.
